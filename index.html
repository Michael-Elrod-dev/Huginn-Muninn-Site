Huginn & Muninn

A personal daily-briefing and reminder system.

Named for Odin's ravens: Huginn (thought) flies out every morning, gathers the day from a handful of sources, and reports back. Muninn (memory) keeps the reminders and fires them.

What it does

Once each morning it assembles a single briefing — the weather, the day's calendar events, upcoming countdowns and any reminders due — and sends it to one Android phone as a notification and a home-screen widget.

Who it is for

One person: the developer. There is no sign-up, no account system, and no public distribution. It is a personal project, not a product.

  • Read-only access to a single Google Calendar
  • No report history — each morning overwrites the last
  • No data shared with anyone
